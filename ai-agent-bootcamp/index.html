Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The guide explains everything in plain English, and AI tools write the code. You'll learn just enough to review changes and fix small things yourself.

Which AI tools do I need to buy?

None to start. Every project can be started on free tiers. The guide explains when a paid plan is worth it, and always shows alternatives. Prices and free tiers change, so check each platform's current pricing.

Does it work on Windows and Mac?

Yes. The tools used are browser-based or available for both. A Chromebook works for most projects.

How long does it take?

Most modules take about 1 to 2 hours. The included 30-day plan breaks everything into roughly 45 to 60 minutes a day, but you can go at your own pace.

Will it go out of date?

AI changes fast, so the guide is built to last: it names platforms instead of specific AI models, describes steps in a durable way instead of click-by-click menus, and links to each platform's official docs.

Is it only for US businesses?

It's written for US small businesses (for example, US sales tax basics and US marketing rules), but most of the skills apply anywhere.

How do I get it?

Right after checkout you'll go straight to a download page with the PDF, starter files, and a quick-start README. You'll also get a Stripe receipt by email.
