Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Introduction

Cody J Web Studio ("we," "our," or "us") is a web development business based in Florida, USA, serving customers in the United States. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or purchase our products.

This policy is written in plain English. It is not a legal contract and does not create any legal obligations beyond those required by applicable law.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

We collect information you voluntarily give us when you:

  • Use our contact form: Name, email address, phone number (optional), subject, and your message. This data is processed by Google Apps Script and stored in our Google Sheets.
  • Purchase a product: When you buy through our Stripe payment links, Stripe collects your payment information. We receive your name, email, and purchase details from Stripe, but we never see or store your full credit card number.
  • Contact us via email or WhatsApp: Whatever information you choose to share in your message.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain information is collected automatically:

  • Analytics data: We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how visitors use our site. This includes pages visited, time on site, browser type, device type, and general location (city/region level). GA4 uses cookies to collect this information. We use Google's Consent Mode, which means analytics tracking only begins after you click "Accept All" on our cookie banner. If you choose "Essential Only," GA4 runs in a limited mode that does not store cookies or collect personal data.
  • Weather demo: Our capabilities page includes a weather widget powered by OpenWeatherMap. If you allow location access in your browser, it sends your coordinates to OpenWeatherMap to display local weather. If you decline, it shows weather for Orlando, FL. Your IP address is also sent to OpenWeatherMap as part of the request.

2.3 Interactive Chat Helper

Our website includes an on-page chat widget. This is a scripted helper that responds to common questions using pre-programmed answers. It runs entirely in your browser and does not send your messages to any external server or store any data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Process and fulfill your purchases
  • Send you order confirmations and download links
  • Improve our website and understand how it's used
  • Protect against fraud and abuse

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect or process your data:

  • Stripe (payments): Processes credit card payments securely. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare (download delivery): Hosts our product download pages. Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics 4 (website analytics): Collects usage data to help us understand how visitors use our site. We have enabled IP anonymization. Google Privacy Policy | GA4 Data Practices
  • Google Apps Script (contact form): Processes form submissions. Google Privacy Policy
  • OpenWeatherMap (weather demo): Provides weather data for our capabilities demo. OpenWeatherMap Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts: Loads fonts for our website. Google Privacy Policy
  • Font Awesome (via cdnjs): Loads icons for our website. Font Awesome Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages (hosting): Hosts our website. GitHub Privacy Statement

5. Cookies

Our website uses cookies for:

  • Essential functionality: Remembering your cookie consent preferences
  • Analytics: Google Analytics 4 cookies to understand site usage (only after you accept analytics cookies)

You can control cookies through your browser settings. For more details, see our Cookie Policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy:

  • Contact form submissions: Retained indefinitely for customer service records unless you request deletion
  • Purchase records: Retained as required for tax and accounting purposes (typically 7 years)
  • Analytics data: Google Analytics 4 retains data according to their retention settings (default 14 months for user data)

7. Your Rights (US Residents)

We do not sell your personal information. Under California law (CCPA) and similar state laws, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect and how we use it
  • Access the personal information we have about you
  • Delete your personal information (with some exceptions)
  • Opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at codyjwebstudio@gmail.com. We will respond within 45 days.

8. Note for EU Visitors

While our business is based in the US and primarily serves US customers, we welcome visitors from anywhere. If you are visiting from the European Union or other regions with specific data protection laws, please be aware that your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. If you have questions or concerns about your rights under GDPR or other applicable laws, please contact us at codyjwebstudio@gmail.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect your information, including HTTPS encryption for all website traffic. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
