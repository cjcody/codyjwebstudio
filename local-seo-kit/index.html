Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be good with computers or marketing?

No. Every step is written in plain English, and any technical term gets a short explanation. If you can fill out a form and use a spreadsheet, you can use this kit.

No, and be careful of anyone who promises that. Rankings depend on things like your location, competition, and reviews. This kit gives you a clear, organized way to cover the fundamentals that local businesses commonly work on. Results vary.

Does it work with Google Sheets, or do I need Excel?

Both. The tracker is a standard Excel file (.xlsx). You can open it in Microsoft Excel, or upload it to Google Sheets for free. Instructions are included.

Is this a physical product?

No. It's a digital download (PDF + spreadsheet). Nothing will be shipped. You can print the checklist at home or at any print shop.
