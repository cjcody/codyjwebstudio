Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You copy the sheet with one click, run setup once, click Allow on Google's permission screens, and use the Business Tools menu. When you want to change something, the guide gives you copy-paste prompts so an AI assistant writes the code change for you.

What do I need?

A Google account (free Gmail works) and a computer for the first setup. After that you can add jobs from the Google Sheets app on your phone. A Stripe account is optional, for "Pay now" links.

Why does Google say the app is not verified?

Google shows that warning for any script a person runs in their own account that has not gone through Google's formal review. Module 1 walks you through the screen step by step and explains every permission. The code runs only inside your own copy of the sheet.

How many emails can it send?

Google sets the limits, not the kit. Free Gmail accounts can email about 100 recipients a day and Google Workspace accounts about 1,500. Google Maps lookups for mileage have their own daily limit. Google can change these, and the guide shows you how to check what you have left.

Does it work with my website?

The contact form snippet works on any site where you can add HTML: GitHub Pages, WordPress (Custom HTML block), Squarespace (Code block) and others. Wix shows it in an embed frame.

Can I change the invoice design?

Yes. Setup creates your invoice, receipt, statement and quote templates as Google Docs. Add your logo, colors and wording. The guide lists every tag you can use.

Is this tax advice?

No. The mileage summary and tax-time expense summary help you organize your records. Check the current IRS mileage rate and the rules for your situation with a tax professional.

How do I get it?

Right after checkout you get the download: the one-click copy link for the ready-made Google Sheet (all the automations are already inside, so there is no code to paste), the templates, the website form and the PDF guide. You also get a Stripe receipt by email.
